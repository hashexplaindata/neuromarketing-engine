# PLATFORM ASPECT RATIOS & SAFE ZONES

## Standard Aspect Ratios
- **Instagram / TikTok Feed**: 9:16 (1080 x 1920 px)
- **Instagram Square**: 1:1 (1080 x 1080 px)
- **YouTube / Desktop Display**: 16:9 (1920 x 1080 px)
- **E-Commerce Product Card**: 4:5 (1080 x 1350 px)
- **Hero Packaging Grid**: 3:2 (1200 x 800 px)

## Critical UI Safe Zones
- **TikTok/Reels Safe Zone**: Top 150px (search/status), Bottom 320px (caption/audio), Right 120px (engagement buttons).
- **Mobile Header Clutter Zone**: Y-coordinates 0.00 to 0.08 normalized height.
- **Thumb Zone Anchor**: X: 0.20 to 0.80, Y: 0.65 to 0.90 for interactive CTA placement.
