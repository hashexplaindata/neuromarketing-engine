## 2. UNIQUE SELLING PROPOSITION: THE N-FACTORIAL ENGINE
Standard A/B testing is mathematically inefficient and reactive. The N-Factorial engine acts as a proactive Autonomous Creative Optimization Engine.

*   **The Workflow:** A user selects components (e.g., 3 backgrounds $\times$ 3 headlines $\times$ 2 buttons = 18 variants).
*   **The Execution:** The platform programmatically stitches all 18 variations in milliseconds via bounding box swapping.
*   **The Value:** It feeds all 18 variants through the pipeline to simulate human eye scanpaths, ranking the output by predictive superiority (e.g., identifying which exact design combination forces the most "First 2-Second Fixations" onto the CTA).

## 3. TARGET PERSONAS & DOMAIN VALUE PROPOSITIONS

### A. UI/UX Designers & Product Teams
*   **The Pain Point:** High cognitive load causes user drop-off in SaaS dashboards and mobile apps.
*   **The Solution:** Evaluates visual entropy and edge complexity. Scores interfaces on a Cognitive Load Score (CLS) to ensure the primary user journey is not buried in visual noise.

### B. Performance Marketers (Image Ads & Creatives)
*   **The Pain Point:** Ad spend is wasted if the brand logo or CTA is ignored in the first 3 seconds of scrolling.
*   **The Solution:** Computes a Brand Attention metric and Visual Clutter Index (VCI) to guarantee the eye naturally flows to the conversion point.

### C. FMCG Brands (Shop Experience & Packaging)
*   **The Pain Point:** Products get lost on crowded retail shelves.
*   **The Solution:** Generates a Shelf Standout Score (SSS) by comparing the saliency of the target product against the surrounding shelf environment (Ratio > 1.5 = Excellent).
